    FINIS TERRAE SAILORS

    SONS OF THE SOUTHERN WINDS

    RESILIENCE AND STRENGTH TO WIN

    The feared Cape Horn, the cold Chiloé fjords, the swell and strong winds of the central coast, and the calm of the North Sea are the schools that have forged the mettle of Chileans: strong and resilient sailors. From the ends of the world, they come to surprise and compete as equals against the great nations of navigators and to inscribe their name in foreign waters. Earthquakes, tsunamis, floods, and droughts. The geographical inclemencies of Chile and the Pacific Ocean are unique, and so are the personalities of its inhabitants; with easy adaptability and resilience, they always manage to get the best out of every situation. The Chilean team is made up of varied personalities that, when complementing each other, transform it into an unbeatable team. With years of experience, eager youngsters, international championships, individual and team sailing, triumphs and defeats, the Chilean team is better prepared to show off on the water.

    The captain

    PABLO LORCA

    As a naval aviator, he is brave, risky, and determined. Our captain began his relationship with the sea in the navy, where he learned discipline and logistics. In addition to being a great bowman, he has managed to form several successful projects: female and mixed crews, as well as match racing championships and corporate regattas. His wide participation in international championships, together with his enormous social skills, makes him “the man” for the job.

    THE LEADERS

    RODRIGO “Rae” AMUNATEGUI

    Methodical and meticulous, our main trimmer takes care of the rig tuning. His “zen” personality contributes in keeping the team calm in and out of the water. From a family of sailors, he began early in IMS boats as a main trimmer on a 40 ft boat and as helmsman on Lightning, J-24, and oceanic ships. He is currently a J-70 tactician and main trimmer in S-40.

     

    Felipe Echenique

    Natural leader. 2 podiums in Optimist World Cups and 2 in South American Laser and Snipe Championships; his experience on board speaks for itself. Calm and gentle on land, hyperkinetic and focused on water. He plans, visualize and eloquently verbalize the maneuvers, anticipating each move. Helmsman and tactician in the high Brazilian leagues.

     

    FELIPE “Flecha” ROBLES

    Dynamic and restless, our helmsman started sailing in dinghy boats, with great results. He began in the J-24 class as helmsman and tactician in different prominent teams, obtaining Pan American Odesur medals and outstanding participation in world championships. He has great technical dialogue and full confidence in the capabilities of his team.

    THE YOUTH FACTOR

    Our young sailors are defined by their effort, perseverance, and hunger for success. They share a desire to compete among the best and bring the energy and ambition of a new generation to the sport. Paula Herman is a perfect example of this spirit. Representing Chile, she claimed silver at the 2019 Lightning Class World Championship in Espoo, Finland, showcasing her talent on the international stage.
