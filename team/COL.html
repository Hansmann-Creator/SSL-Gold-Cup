    GOLDEN COFFEE

    GOLDEN COFFEE

    Colombia is the northernmost tip of South America between the Pacific Ocean and the Caribbean Sea. It is one of the most bioverse countries in the world and has many cultures centered around water.

    Sailing happens at sea level and lakes above 2,600 meters, in all kinds of weather and temperatures. The Colombian Sailing Federation was founded in 1958, since then the sport has grown mainly around lightning, snipe, sunfish, ilca and optimist classes. Both wind surfing and kite have become increasingly popular.

    Sailors in the young Colombian team come from these classes, are active in Colombia and around the world. Many of them know each other from their optimist days and trust that their friendship will help them build a very powerful SSL team.

    THE CAPTAIN

    Simon Gomez

    Colombian sailor dedicated 100% to competitive sailing since the age of 12. Outstanding multi-class sailor (Ilca, Sunfish, Wingfoil, Iqfoil). Among his most notable results are: Bolivarian Champion in Optimist and Sunfish, North American Sunfish Champion, two-time World Junior Sunfish Champion, 5th place in the Pan American Games in Iqfoil. Eleven-time national champion in multiple classes.

    THE LEADERS

    GUSTAVO TAMAYO

    Gustavo Tamayo is one of Colombia’s leading Lightning sailors, having represented his country at numerous international events. Beyond competition, he is a passionate advocate for the class, helping bring major events such as the South American and World Championships to Colombia. He is also committed to developing young talent, supporting aspiring sailors and creating opportunities for them to compete at a higher level.

    Andrey Quintero

    Andrey Quintero competed at the 2012 London Olympics and is now coaching the Colombian ILCA team. A five-time Pan American Games athlete, he has also won medals on the Olympic circuit and regional games. He is a pioneer of keelboat sailing in Colombia.

    Ana Sofia Bermudez

    Ana Sofia is an accomplished Sunfish sailor who has won several international medals in the class and represented her country in the Sunfish event at the 2023 Pan American Games in Santiago.

    THE YOUTH FACTOR

    Our team in one of the youngest team, proving and demostrating a bright future for the colombian sailing comunity, hopfully inspiring more young sailors to dedicate to the sport and high level performance.
