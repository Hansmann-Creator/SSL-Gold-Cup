    HIGHER AND FASTER

    All you need is Ecuador

    Ecuador is a country with a very diverse geography, we sail between the Enchanted Islands of Galapagos, paradisiacal coasts and in the Andes in lakes between volcanoes. Despite being a small country, we have achieved world and continental titles in several classes. Competing in this event gives us another opportunity to show that we are always ready for difficult challenges. We consider that a strength of the team is unity. Many of us learned to sail and compete together as children.

    The captain

    JULIO VÉLEZ 

    Julio Vélez is an Ecuadorian sailor with more than 30 years of competitive sailing experience. Throughout his career, he has represented Ecuador in numerous national and international championships, highlighted by winning the South American Championship in the Lightning Class. He currently serves as skipper of a Lightning team and a Soto 39, where he brings his experience, leadership, and strategic vision to high-level competition. Beyond racing, Julio is committed to the development of sailing, actively supporting the growth of future generations of sailors and contributing to the advancement of the sport in Ecuador.

    THE LEADERS

    JONATHAN MARTINETTI 

    Jonathan Martinetti is an Ecuadorian sailor widely regarded as one of the most accomplished athletes in the history of sailing in Ecuador. He began his sailing career at the age of 11 at Puerto Lucía Yacht Club and has built an outstanding international career. Among his most notable achievements are the gold medal at the 2015 Toronto Pan American Games, two Sunfish World Championship titles (2010 and 2021), the 2023 Lightning World Championship title, and the Sunfish World Championship runner-up finish in the same year. In addition, he has won multiple youth world titles, as well as numerous South American and national championships across different sailing classes.

    THE YOUTH FACTOR

    The young Ecuadorian generation of sailors is growing over the years. They are talented and have a lot of determination. They have passion and are motivated to improve every day, and they share experiences and learn from the best sailors in Ecuador. Most of them sail Olympic and Pan American boats and dream of becoming an Olympic medallist.
