    VANN SWET

    FINIS CORONAT OPUS

    SEY Coat of Arms: “The End Crowns The Work”

    With no indigenous people, the Seychelles was found by sailors, Vasco de Gama and 4th Portuguese India armada in 1503, and settled by small groups from Mauritius and ReUnion. The Seychelles life lines have always been boats, until the airport was built in 1972.

    Schooners were the traditional island suppliers, small pirogues for fishing the main food source. The Seychellois have been on and in the water from the very start. The flying fish logo represents that confirmation that it’s going to be a great day on the water, no matter how often you see flying fish, they are always a spectacle and one finds themselves following their flights when ever spotted.

    ACHIEVEMENTS

    • 5 Olympic Games participated (Allan Julie: 1996, 2000, 2004, 2008, 2016)
    • 1 All-Africa Games gold medal (Allan Julie, 2011 Maputo)
    • 20+ years Olympic qualification streak (2000-2020+ consecutive Olympic sailing)
    • 1 All-Africa Games gold medal (Allan Julie, 2011 Maputo)

    THE CAPTAIN

    Allan Julie

    Allan Julie stands as one of the most accomplished athletes in Seychelles’ sporting history, representing the island nation with distinction across an extraordinary 20-year Olympic career. Born on March 23, 1977, this exceptional sailor has become synonymous with Seychellois excellence on the world’s waters.

     

     

     

     

     

     

    THE LEADERS

    Danny Hugh Adeline

    Danny Hugh Adeline compete in windsurfing at the 1992 Barcelona Olympic Games, representing Seychelles.

    Henri Dauban

    Henri Dauban de Silhouette competed in the men’s javelin throw at the 1924 Summer Olympics.

    Roland Raforme

    1992 Barcelona Olympics, Roland Raforme (Light Heavyweight) – Seychelles’ flag bearer and best Olympic performance

     

     

     

     

     

     

    THE YOUTH FACTOR

    Seychelles entry into the SSL GOLD CUP is being considered the next great hope to kick start professional sailing in this tiny nation. With drive and impetus to compete on the international stage we are hoping that the sailing spark in the general public will be reignited and our history on the water will be reinvigorated.
