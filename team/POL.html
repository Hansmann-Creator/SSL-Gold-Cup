    SEA WOLVES

    Inspire young generation

    Aspiring to victory

    Poland has a long sailing tradition. Access to the sea and many lakes including Masurian Lakeland makes watersports accessible for almost half a million Polish sailors. A sport learned in their childhood, Poles love sailing. Polish fortitude and skills make us want to win at sea and conquer the oceans. Krystyna Chojnowska-Liskiewicz was the first woman to circumnavigate the Earth. Polish sailors are experienced Olympians, Match Racers, America’s Cup and offshore sailors. The young generation is coming through strong and they are aspiring for International victories. Teamwork and mutual help is a strength of this team. The SSL Team Poland wants to improve, keep learning and be better every day to leave their mark on the world of international fleet sailing for future generations.

    The Captain of SSL Team Poland – Mateusz Kusznierewicz is the ISAF Sailor of the Year.

    THE CAPTAIN

    Mateusz Kusznierewicz

    Mateusz won the first ever Olympic medal in the history of Polish sailing and together he has two Olympic medals: Gold from Atlanta and Bronze from Athens. He is a four-time World Champion, European Champion in OK-Dinghy, Finn, Star and 5.5 class, as well as a podium winner in the most prestigious regattas in the world: Star Sailors League, Princess Sophia Trophy, Kieler Woche, Bacardi Cup, Palma Vela and many more.

    THE LEADERS

    Sławosz Uznański – Wiśniewski

    Poland’s first astronaut, joins Team Poland as a mentor and source of inspiration. Widely admired in Poland, Sławosz represents ambition, perseverance and the courage to reach beyond the limits. His passion for sailing, with experience in Laser and J/70, also brings valuable knowledge and perspective to the team. While officially presented as part of Team Poland, his most important role is to inspire and support the crew, sharing his experience and mindset of striving for excellence.

    THE YOUTH FACTOR

    The young generation of Polish sailors are talented and extraordinary people with great passion. They have passion and are motivated to improve everyday and learn from the best. Most of them sail Olympic boats and compete in match racing and offshore regattas.
