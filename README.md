# Gold Cup Rio Matchboard

Unofficial companion app for the SSL Gold Cup Rio 2026 (Copa do Mundo da Vela): rounds, matchboard, qualifying series, teams and sailors in English, German, French and Spanish.

- Results are read from the official results page (https://sslgoldcup.worldofsailors.com/results/) every 10 minutes between 11:00 and 19:00 Rio time on race days by the GitHub Action in `.github/workflows/update.yml`.
- Race times come from the official schedule (https://sslgoldcup.worldofsailors.com/schedule/).
- Logos, fonts and sailor portraits belong to the SSL / event and are used for this event companion only.
